SOLVD EMAIL SIGNATURE GENERATOR
================================

STEP 1: UNZIP (if needed)
- If you received a .zip file, double-click it to unzip
- You'll see a file called generate-signature.command

STEP 2: ALLOW THE FILE TO RUN
- Double-click generate-signature.command
- macOS will show a warning and block it
- Open System Settings > Privacy & Security
- Scroll down — you'll see a message about generate-signature.command being blocked
- Click "Allow Anyway"
- Go back and double-click generate-signature.command again
- Click "Open" when prompted
- You only need to do this once

STEP 3: FILL IN YOUR DETAILS
A Terminal window will open and ask for your details:

- Full name — type your name, press Enter
- Title — e.g. "Group Consultant - Client Services", press Enter
- Email — must be your @solvdagency.com.au address, press Enter
- Phone — your mobile number starting with 04 (or leave blank to skip), press Enter
- LinkedIn — by default this links to the Solvd company page.
  Type 'y' if you want to use your personal LinkedIn URL instead.

STEP 4: CHECK YOUR DETAILS
- The script will show a summary of what you entered
- Type Y (or just press Enter) to confirm
- Type N to start over

STEP 5: USE YOUR SIGNATURE
- Your signature is saved to your Desktop and copied to your clipboard
- A preview will open in your browser so you can check it

For Gmail:
  1. Open Gmail
  2. Click the gear icon > See all settings
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section
  4. Click "Create new"
  5. Paste (Cmd+V) into the signature box
  6. Scroll down and click Save Changes

For Apple Mail:
  1. Open Mail > Settings > Signatures
  2. Click the + to create a new signature
  3. Open the HTML file from your Desktop in Safari
  4. Select all (Cmd+A), copy (Cmd+C)
  5. Paste into the Mail signature editor
